# Design Constitution v1.1 - Restored Organelle Economy

## Core law

Bodies do not attack by default. Organs attack. The v1.1.1 starter has no acid action. Acid chemistry requires the late Tier 2 Toxin Sac, so the early game remains scavenger-first.

## World law

The world remains a vertical oxygen ecology:

- Surface: light, oxygen, photosynthetic algae growth.
- Nursery: anaerobic scavenger play.
- Rupture layer: sinking algae meet predators.
- Depth: exotic resource and eventual DNA information.

## Feeding law

Matter is field-based. Biomass, lipids, toxins, and energy are absorbed from fields by overlap and feeding flow. Spores, exotic traces, and DNA remain discrete particles.

## Metabolism law

Starter cells ferment biomass into ATP/energy automatically, producing toxic waste. Fermentation is not a menu exchange; it is a body process. The Fermentation Matrix upgrades this process.

Low ATP must matter. Movement and upkeep spend energy, and low energy slows locomotion.

## Storage law

Storage is embodied. Capacity increases come from vacuoles/vesicle racks, not abstract inventory upgrades.

- Storage Vacuole Cluster: biomass, lipids, toxins, ATP/ATP.
- Exotic Vesicle Rack: spores, enzymes, crystals, vacuole seeds, DNA records.

## Combat law

Combat organs are visible and physically interpretable:

- Rasping Lamella: active overlap shred.
- Lance Bristle: directional contact/reach weapon, strongest when aligned.
- Toxin Sac/Cloud: stronger chemical specialization.

## Eucharist law

Mitochondria are not purchased as a normal organelle. Host-worthiness opens Yuki's Eucharist. After integration, oxygen and lipids become high-energy metabolism and Tier 3 DNA information opens.

## Frontend law

The frontend renders and routes input. The graph kernel owns rules: resources, caps, organelles, actions, costs, metabolism, death bloom, and progression.
