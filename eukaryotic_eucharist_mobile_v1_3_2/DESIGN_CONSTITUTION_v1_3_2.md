# Design Constitution v1.3.2 — Flow and Ingestion Balance

## Core law

Nothing exists without a graph source. Every stat, capacity, action, damage source, and metabolic transform must be justified by organelle counts.

## Matter / energy flow law

Bulk resource use is curved, not flat.

- Full tanks produce larger flow volume.
- Full tanks are less efficient.
- Nearly empty tanks are slower but more frugal.
- This applies first to biomass → ATP through Anaerobic Processors and is the template for movement/metabolism curves.

## Ingestion law

Matter fields are consumed by feeding. Exotic particles are not coins. Spores, enzymes, crystals, and DNA must be deliberately ingested with a feeding organ active and available storage.

Particles must remain in the world if the body has no matching storage room.

## Toxicity law

Toxins are an internal hazard, not free cargo. Above safe tank levels, toxin overload damages the body and emits a green stress cue. Oxygen overload remains blue; both can coexist.

## Predator law

Since soft-body collision separation is removed, predatory AI must aim for center-overlap, not edge contact. Rasping damage is a function of overlap; predators should stand on prey to rupture them.

## Ecology pressure law

The mature initial ecosystem is not enough. The running simulation must continue to seed algae falls, predators, rupture opportunities, and scavenger pressure.
