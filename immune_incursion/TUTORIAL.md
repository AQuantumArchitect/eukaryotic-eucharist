EE is the dense-tick world where the hive learns to chase three named scores
with Umwelt as witness. Transfer that mapping later; do not copy organelles
into SpaceWheat or mail.

| Score | What it is | Referee |
|---|---|---|
| **Truth** | Graph-strict. Command = organ. Sensors bind spec nodes. | `apply("fun")` is a no-op. `umwelt.spec.validate` when Umwelt is lit. |
| **Beauty** | Strange attractors / common modes. | Umwelt `score_breathing`: golden, multiplicity, return_count; not flat. Lab 0 unmixes the ecology champion. Immune breath is Lab 2. |
| **Goodness** | Engaging gate, not mixed into beauty. | No storm, no collapse, at least one budgeted clear. |
