# Eukaryotic Eucharist — Immune Incursion Constitution

The bacteria game. Not Mecha Fish. Player-facing name stays **Eukaryotic
Eucharist**. This sitting pins law only. No playable zip.

## Core sentence

You are the host immune system. Pathogens breach tissue. You fight them with
organs. The loop is incursions.

## Four verbs

1. **Detect** — an incursion has a breach, a caste, and a signal.
2. **Deploy** — activate or grow immune organs (the old bacteria tooling).
3. **Clear** — end the wave without losing the tissue bed.
4. **Remember** — keep a DNA / recipe trace so the next incursion is not identical.

## Player

The player is not a swimming scavenger in a water column. The player is the
host's immune apparatus: a body-graph of organs acting on a tissue bed.
Graph-strict still holds: every function is an organelle; bodies attack only
through organs.

## Incursion loop

An incursion is the whole game cycle, not a side mode.

1. **Breach** — pathogens enter a tissue bed from an edge or wound.
2. **Wave** — a caste mix arrives. It has a budget, not an infinite spawn.
3. **Fight** — player tools vs invaders. Overlap, fields, and organs — not
   click-damage.
4. **Resolve** — clear, or fail.
5. **Memory** — a cleared strain can leave a recipe (RNA/DNA). The next
   incursion may reuse, mutate, or escalate that strain.

Between incursions the tissue bed is a rest / graft beat (lymph node), not an
open trophic column.

## Fail states (both count)

- **Tissue collapse** — the bed's HP / structure hits zero. Incursion wins.
- **Cytokine storm** — player over-deploys toxins / inflammation and kills the
  host. Over-response is a real loss, not a flex.
