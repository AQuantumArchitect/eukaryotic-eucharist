## Core sentence

You are a mechanical fish in a vertical water column. You survive by swimming
the gradients, feeding tanks from fields, and grafting organs onto the hull.

## Four verbs

1. **Swim** — position in oxygen / light / current.
2. **Feed** — absorb matter from fields, not coins.
3. **Graft** — grow organs that change what the hull can do.
4. **Bloom** — death becomes terrain: wreck fields feed the column.
